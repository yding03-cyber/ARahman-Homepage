I am a third-year PhD candidate at the **UC Berkeley Speech & Audio Lab**. My research focuses on **speech processing and audio machine learning**. I graduated from the University of Dhaka in October 2021.

## Research Interests

- Low-Resource Speech Recognition
- Neural Speech Synthesis
- Self-Supervised Audio Representation Learning
